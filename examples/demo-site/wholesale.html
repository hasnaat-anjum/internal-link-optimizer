Wholesale Coffee

We supply wholesale coffee beans to cafes, restaurants and offices, with flexible volumes from 5kg a week upwards. Our wholesale customers get the same fresh roast as our retail bags, plus barista training and brewing equipment support.

Our Colombian beans are the workhorse of many cafe espresso menus, while the Ethiopian Yirgacheffe shines as a filter option. Talk to us about a tasting session for your team.

Wholesale orders are roasted to order every Monday and delivered within 48 hours. Get in touch for a price list and a sample bag.
