Brewing Guide

Good coffee starts with fresh beans and the right grind. For pour over, use a medium-fine grind and water just off the boil. Bloom the grounds for 45 seconds, then pour slowly in circles.

French press is the most forgiving method: coarse grind, four minutes of steeping, and a slow press. It suits our Colombian beans perfectly, bringing out their chocolate sweetness.

Cold brew takes patience — twelve hours of steeping — but rewards you with a smooth, low-acid coffee that keeps for a week in the fridge. Use a coarse grind and a 1:8 coffee-to-water ratio.

Whichever method you choose, grind your beans just before brewing. Pre-ground coffee loses its aroma within days, while whole beans stay fresh for weeks.
