Our Coffee Beans

Every bag starts as a green coffee bean from a farm we know by name. Our Ethiopian Yirgacheffe comes from high-altitude farms and tastes of jasmine and bergamot. Our Colombian Huila is a crowd favourite: sweet, chocolatey and forgiving to brew.

Prefer coffee without the caffeine? Our Swiss Water decaf keeps the full flavour of the bean with 99.9% of the caffeine removed. Decaf drinkers can still enjoy a proper pour over in the morning.

Not sure which beans suit your taste? Try a tasting flight of three small bags and find your favourite roast before you commit to a full bag. Our brewing guide shows you how to get the best from each roast, whatever method you use.
